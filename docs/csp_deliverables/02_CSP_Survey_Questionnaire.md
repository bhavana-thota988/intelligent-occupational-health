# COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT (CSP) SURVEY QUESTIONNAIRE
## Workplace Health, Postural Strain & Wellbeing Screening Instrument

**Project Title**: Intelligent Occupational Health and Workplace Risk Management Platform  
**Target Community**: Sedentary Desk Workers, Office Personnel, and Remote Employees  
**Standard Compliance**: Aligned with ISO 45001 (Workplace Health & Safety) and OSHA Ergonomic Guidelines  

---

### Introduction & Consent Note
*This survey instrument is designed to evaluate occupational risk factors associated with prolonged seated computer work. It captures habitual workday behaviors across physical posture, sedentary duration, cognitive stress, and recovery. Responses are confidential and utilized solely for non-diagnostic wellbeing education and personalized intervention mapping.*

### Part C: Risk Scoring Methodology & Tier Classification

Each response option carries a standardized score from **1 (Optimal/Low Risk)** to **4 (Severe Risk)**.  

$$\text{Raw Category Score} = \sum \text{Item Weights}$$

$$\text{Normalized Dimension Percentage} = \frac{\text{Raw Score} - \text{Min Possible}}{\text{Max Possible} - \text{Min Possible}} \times 100\%$$

* **Overall Score < 35%**: **Low Risk (Optimal Workplace Health)**
* **Overall Score 35% – 64%**: **Moderate Risk (Targeted Intervention Advised)**
* **Overall Score $\ge$ 65%**: **High Risk (Immediate Ergonomic Intervention Required)**
