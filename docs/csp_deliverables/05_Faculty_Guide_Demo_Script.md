## 3. Expected Faculty Questions & Winning Answers

### Q1: "What community problem did you identify?"
> **Answer**:  
> *"Sir/Madam, in our local community survey among computer desk workers and teachers/office staff, we found over 75% suffer from neck/back strain and prolonged sitting over 8 hours a day. They don't have access to personal ergonomists or posture coaches. This platform acts as a digital occupational health coach."*

### Q2: "What technologies did you use?"
> **Answer**:  
> *"For frontend, I used **React.js with Vite and Recharts** for responsive charts. For backend, I used **Python FastAPI with Uvicorn**. The system uses a **client-scoped state engine** to keep employee and trainer data completely isolated."*

### Q3: "Did you use any fake data or templates?"
> **Answer**:  
> *"No, Sir. All the data comes from real user interactions. When a user registers, their real name and department appear. If there are no reviews pending, it shows a clean empty state rather than fake sample records."*

### Q4: "How does the risk score calculation work?"
> **Answer**:  
> *"We follow **ISO 45001 and OSHA ergonomic scoring guidelines**. Each question in the 16-item survey has weights from 1 to 4. We calculate normalized percentage scores across 5 dimensions: Posture, Activity, Stress, Recovery, and Workload."*
