# COMMUNITY SERVICE PROJECT (CSP) ABSTRACT

## Title of the Project
**Intelligent Occupational Health and Workplace Risk Management Platform**
*Sub-title: Workplace Health, Risk Assessment & Employee Wellbeing Management*

---

### Abstract

Modern sedentary workplace environments, prolonged screen exposure, and high-intensity cognitive workloads have precipitated an alarming surge in Work-Related Musculoskeletal Disorders (WMSDs), postural misalignment, chronic fatigue, and psychological burnout. In community workplaces—ranging from IT corridors and educational institutions to corporate office clusters—employees frequently lack accessible, personalized, and non-diagnostic ergonomic guidance to mitigate these emerging health hazards before they escalate into debilitating clinical conditions.

To address this pressing community health challenge, the **Intelligent Occupational Health and Workplace Risk Management Platform** was engineered as an evidence-based digital occupational health ecosystem. The platform bridges the gap between individual risk identification and clinical intervention through an integrated, continuous operational workflow structured across two dedicated workspaces: the **Employee Workspace** and the **Trainer/Ergonomist Workspace**.

The platform incorporates an ISO 45001 and OSHA-aligned 16-factor screening instrument that systematically evaluates employee habits across four distinct workday phases (Morning, Work, Breaks, and Evening) spanning five primary risk dimensions: **Posture & Musculoskeletal Strain**, **Sedentary Duration**, **Cognitive Stress**, **Circadian Recovery**, and **Workload Cadence**. Based on multi-axial risk calculations and interactive radar visualization, the system dynamically maps employees to one of five official corporate wellbeing curricula: *Workplace Ergonomics & Posture*, *Workplace Yoga & Mobility*, *Daily Movement & Stretch Breaks*, *Guided Meditation & Stress Reset*, or *Healthy Recovery & Sleep Habits*.

To ensure accountability and prevent self-awarded or unverified claims, the platform implements a clinical verification gateway. Employees log daily structured workstation drills through interactive checklists; upon 100% curriculum completion, the program is submitted for review to an accredited occupational coach. Trainers evaluate adherence using a 4-point verification checklist (Completion, Consistency, Reflection, and Ergonomic Alignment), affix specialized clinical observations, and issue a cryptographic, uniquely numbered certificate of workplace health compliance (`IOH-2026-XXXXXX`) that is publicly verifiable.

Developed utilizing **React.js, Vite, and Recharts** for the responsive frontend interface, **Python FastAPI and Uvicorn** for high-throughput microservices, and a secure client-side scoped persistence engine, the platform demonstrates how technology-assisted occupational screening and guided rehabilitation can foster healthier, resilient workplace communities with zero admin overhead and high operational fidelity.
